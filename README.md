# Ruiyi Zhou

### 🌐 [Academic website — everything about me](https://ruiyiqm.github.io/)

[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=LnlwJG8AAAAJ) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-7732-6338)

I am a theoretical chemist and a postdoctoral researcher with Jeremy Richardson at ETH Zürich. I develop new first-principles methods for electronic excited states and non-adiabatic dynamics of molecules and materials.

My research mainly includes three directions:

- **Many-body excited states:** all-electron GW and Bethe–Salpeter equation (BSE) methods for periodic systems, including dynamical BSE.
- **Nonequilibrium electron dynamics:** real-time TDDFT and coupled electron–proton dynamics.
- **Excited-state dynamics and reaction rates:** nonadiabatic dynamics with MASH, and first-principles electronic structure combined with golden-rule instanton theory.

### Current development

- **[TDMASH_pack](https://github.com/ruiyiQM/TDMASH_pack)** — RT-TDDFT-driven MASH for nonadiabatic excited-state dynamics.
- **[LibBSE](https://github.com/ruiyiQM/LibBSE)** — a periodic BSE solver built on top of FHI-aims and ABACUS.
- **[ExciView](https://github.com/ruiyiQM/ExciView)** — BSE and excited-state analysis tools for FHI-aims and libBSE.
- **[ABACUS development fork](https://github.com/ruiyiQM/abacus-develop)** — frozen-density embedding in ABACUS.
